PHARMACEUTICAL SUPPLY AND ACCESS AGREEMENT (LOWER-RISK VERSION)

Agreement Reference: SYNTH-PHARMA-2026-002
Date: February 1, 2026
Parties:
  - Supplier: BetterPharma Solutions LLC (a fictitious entity)
  - Buyer:    Lakeside Regional Health Network (a fictitious entity)

---

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Products" means the pharmaceutical compounds listed in Schedule A, including
Compound Z-12 and associated biologic therapies.

1.2 "Material Deviation" means any confirmed departure from agreed quality or
safety specifications that may affect patient safety or regulatory compliance status.

1.3 "Escalation Event" means any supply disruption exceeding three (3) business
days that triggers mandatory notification and remediation obligations.

---

2. SCOPE OF AGREEMENT

2.1 BetterPharma Solutions LLC agrees to supply Compound Z-12 and associated
pharmaceutical products to Lakeside Regional Health Network under the terms
set forth in this agreement.

2.2 This agreement covers procurement, delivery, storage, and compliance for a
term of two (2) years commencing from the date of signature.

---

3. PRICING

3.1 The initial unit price for Compound Z-12 shall be $3,500 per unit as of the
effective date.

3.2 Pricing Review Process: Prices may be reviewed annually on the anniversary of
the effective date. Any proposed price adjustment must be formally submitted to the
Buyer at least ninety (90) days in advance of the proposed effective date.

3.3 Annual price adjustments are capped at a maximum of five percent (5%) above
the prior year's unit price, indexed to the U.S. Consumer Price Index (CPI).

3.4 Any proposed adjustment exceeding the CPI cap requires written consent of both
parties prior to taking effect.

---

4. REGULATORY COMPLIANCE AND DEVIATION REPORTING

4.1 Both parties agree to maintain compliance with all applicable federal, state,
and local regulations governing pharmaceutical supply and patient care.

4.2 Deviation Reporting: The supplier shall report material deviations within
fifteen (15) days of discovery. For deviations that may immediately affect patient
safety or treatment availability, notification must occur within twenty-four (24)
hours.

4.3 Deviation reports shall include root cause analysis, corrective action plan,
and expected resolution timeline.

4.4 Failure to report within the specified window constitutes a material breach
of this agreement.

---

5. DELIVERY AND SUPPLY OBLIGATIONS

5.1 BetterPharma Solutions LLC commits to delivering Products within fourteen (14)
calendar days of confirmed order receipt under normal operating conditions.

5.2 Supply Continuity: In the event that Products become temporarily unavailable
due to manufacturing or logistics disruption, the Supplier shall notify the Buyer
within forty-eight (48) hours of the anticipated disruption.

5.3 Escalation procedure: If delivery is delayed by more than five (5) business
days, the Supplier must provide a written remediation plan within three (3)
business days, including a substitution option or alternative source if available.

5.4 Chronic failure is defined as three (3) or more delivery failures within a
sixty (60) day period. Chronic failure triggers the Buyer's right to source
alternative supply at Supplier's cost, capped at a reasonable market premium.

---

6. AUDIT RIGHTS

6.1 Right to Audit: The Buyer shall have the right to audit the Supplier's
manufacturing facilities, quality records, and compliance documentation once per
calendar year, with thirty (30) days advance written notice.

6.2 Extraordinary audits may be conducted within seven (7) business days notice
if a material deviation, regulatory action, or patient safety event has been
identified.

6.3 The Supplier shall provide full access to all relevant records, personnel, and
facilities required for audit completion.

---

7. LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

7.1 Each party shall indemnify and hold harmless the other from claims arising from
their own negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of this agreement.

7.2 Liability Cap: The maximum aggregate liability of either party under this
agreement shall not exceed the total contract value paid or payable in the twelve
(12) months preceding the event giving rise to the claim.

7.3 Consequential damages are excluded except in cases of fraud, gross negligence,
or willful breach.

7.4 The liability cap shall be reviewed and updated annually in connection with
the pricing review process described in Section 3.

---

8. TERM AND TERMINATION

8.1 This agreement commences February 1, 2026, and continues for two (2) years
unless terminated earlier pursuant to this Section.

8.2 Termination for Convenience: Either party may terminate this agreement for
convenience by providing ninety (90) days prior written notice.

8.3 During any termination notice period, both parties shall fulfill outstanding
delivery obligations and maintain supply continuity to protect patient access.

8.4 Termination for Cause: Either party may terminate immediately upon written
notice if the other party commits a material breach and fails to cure within
thirty (30) days of notice.

---

9. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

9.1 This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois.

9.2 Disputes shall be resolved through escalating negotiation, then mediation,
then binding arbitration under the AAA Commercial Rules.
