  - Buyer:    Metro General Hospital System (a fictitious entity)

---

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Products" means the pharmaceutical compounds listed in Schedule A, including
Compound X-47 and related biologics.

1.2 "Delivery Period" means the period within which Products must be delivered to
the Buyer's designated receiving facility.

1.3 "Material Deviation" means any departure from agreed quality, quantity, or
compliance specifications that may affect product safety or regulatory status.

---

2. SCOPE OF AGREEMENT

2.1 SynthPharma Inc. agrees to supply Compound X-47 and associated pharmaceutical
products to Metro General Hospital System under the terms and conditions set forth
herein.

2.2 This agreement covers procurement, delivery, storage, and compliance obligations
for a term of three (3) years commencing from the date of signature.

---

3. PRICING

3.1 The initial unit price for Compound X-47 shall be $4,200 per unit as of the
effective date of this agreement.

3.2 Prices may change at any time based on market conditions, raw material costs,
or manufacturing expenses. The Supplier reserves the right to adjust pricing without
a defined cap or advance notice period.

3.3 The Buyer acknowledges that pricing is subject to adjustment at Supplier's
sole discretion in response to market conditions, including supply chain volatility
and regulatory costs.

3.4 No maximum annual adjustment percentage is defined in this agreement.

---

4. REGULATORY COMPLIANCE AND DEVIATION REPORTING

4.1 Both parties agree to comply with applicable federal, state, and local regulations
governing the manufacture, supply, and use of pharmaceutical products.

4.2 Deviation Reporting: The supplier shall report material deviations within ninety
days of discovery. This includes quality control failures, batch recall triggers, and
regulatory non-conformance events identified during manufacturing or delivery.

4.3 For deviations considered non-material by the Supplier's internal quality team,
no reporting obligation applies unless the Buyer formally requests disclosure.

---

5. DELIVERY AND SUPPLY OBLIGATIONS

5.1 SynthPharma Inc. shall use commercially reasonable efforts to deliver Products
within the agreed lead times.

5.2 All deliveries are subject to availability. In the event that Products are not
available due to production constraints, force majeure events, or supply chain
disruptions, SynthPharma Inc. shall notify the Buyer at its earliest convenience.

5.3 No escalation timeline or substitution process is defined for supply interruption
events. The Buyer accepts that delivery schedules may be adjusted without a defined
remediation or contingency pathway.

5.4 In the event of chronic delivery failure, the Buyer's sole remedy is to submit
a written complaint. No penalties, substitution rights, or breach remedies are
specified for repeated delivery failures.

---

6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

6.1 All formulations, manufacturing processes, and proprietary data related to
Compound X-47 remain the exclusive intellectual property of SynthPharma Inc.

6.2 The Buyer shall not reverse-engineer, disclose, or use any proprietary
information for any purpose other than patient care as defined in Schedule B.

---

7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Both parties agree to maintain the confidentiality of all proprietary and
commercially sensitive information shared under this agreement for a period of
five (5) years following expiration or termination.

---

8. TERM AND TERMINATION

8.1 This agreement shall commence on January 15, 2026, and continue for a period
of three (3) years unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Section.

8.2 Either party may terminate this agreement for convenience by providing written
notice. No minimum notice period is specified for termination for convenience.

8.3 Immediate termination without cause is permitted if either party determines
that continuation of the agreement is no longer commercially viable.

---

9. LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

9.1 Each party shall indemnify and hold harmless the other party from and against
claims arising from their own negligence or willful misconduct.

9.2 SynthPharma Inc. shall not be liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive
damages arising from delivery delays, quality failures, or regulatory actions.

9.3 No cap on liability is defined in this agreement. Both parties acknowledge that
maximum financial exposure under this agreement is unlimited to the extent permitted
by applicable law.

---

10. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

10.1 This agreement shall be governed by the laws of the State of New York.

10.2 Any dispute arising under this agreement shall first be subject to good-faith
negotiation, followed by binding arbitration under the rules of the American
Arbitration Association.
